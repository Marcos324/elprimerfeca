Selected protagonist story:

The Justicia bonaerense suspended for 60 days the application of the new Regimen Penal Juvenil in Provincia de Buenos Aires, two days after the national law entered into force and the day it was reglamented by Decreto 875/2026. The law lowers criminal responsibility age from 16 to 14 under a specific juvenile system. Judge Marta Elba Pascual, Juzgado de Responsabilidad Penal Juvenil Nro. 2 de Lomas de Zamora, accepted a preventive collective habeas corpus and framed the issue around whether the province has the material, institutional, and professional conditions to absorb more detained adolescents without violating rights. Government figures criticized the decision and pushed the public-safety frame.
