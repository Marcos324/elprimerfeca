# Cover Prompt Template

Generate a vertical 9:16 illustrated editorial cover for an COFFEE & TV Instagram Reel.

## Title

`[SHORT UPPERCASE TITLE]`

## Visual Direction

The cover should look like one coherent generated editorial artwork, not a banner pasted over a frame.

Include:

- COFFEE & TV brand language and logo reference;
- `[CHARACTER]` when useful;
- one strong visual metaphor from the story;
- warm Buenos Aires morning newsroom texture or the story-specific location;
- high-contrast title centered in the square-safe crop.

## Story Context

`[WHAT HAPPENED AND WHY IT MATTERS]`

## Constraints

- Title must be large, readable, centered and integrated into the art.
- Avoid subtitle UI, black rectangles, flat lower thirds, sticker collage or Canva-like overlays.
- Avoid too much small text.
- Make the thumbnail legible at phone size.
